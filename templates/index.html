Project Background

Why this platform matters

Choosing a university can be confusing because important information is often spread across different websites. Students may need to search separately for majors, admission requirements, transfer credits, tuition, scholarships, and support services. This can be especially difficult for first-generation students, international students, and families who are learning how the college process works.

This website organizes reliable information from Arizona universities into a structured format so users can make informed decisions with less stress and fewer scattered browser tabs, because apparently college planning was not chaotic enough already.

Target Audience

Built for students and families

Arizona High School Students

Students exploring four-year universities, academic programs, admissions steps, and future career pathways.

Transfer Students

Students beginning at community colleges who need clear transfer pathways into bachelor’s degree programs.

First-Generation & International Students

Students who may need extra guidance understanding university systems, costs, requirements, and support programs.

Parents & Guardians

Families helping with educational planning, financial decisions, and university comparison.
