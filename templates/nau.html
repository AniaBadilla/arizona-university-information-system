Transfer Pathways

Community college to university transfer support

Transfer pathways are especially important for Arizona students who begin at community colleges before moving to a four-year university. NAU provides transfer guides and credit evaluation tools that help students understand how completed coursework applies toward a bachelor’s degree.

Helpful for:

  • Community college transfer students
  • First-generation college students
  • Working adult learners
  • International transfer students
  • Students changing majors or institutions
