Admissions

Application information for different student types

Admissions information is organized for first-year students, transfer students, international students, and online learners. Students may need to submit an application, academic transcripts, and supporting documents depending on their student type.

Students are encouraged to apply by the Early Action deadline of November 1, especially for scholarship consideration. FAFSA completion is also recommended because it helps students access financial aid opportunities.

Transfer Pathways

Planning from community college to university

Transfer students can use University of Arizona transfer tools to understand how previous coursework may apply toward a bachelor’s degree. This supports smoother transitions for students moving from community colleges or other accredited institutions into university programs.

Helpful for:

  • Community college transfer students
  • First-generation students
  • International transfer students
  • Students changing academic pathways
